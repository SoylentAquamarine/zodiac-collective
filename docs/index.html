Our first goal is a defensible cryptanalytic determination of Z13 — either a reproducible solution or a rigorous account of why one isn't possible without a crib. Our second is to document every step here. Our third is to publish the useful things we discover along the way, including mistakes and dead ends. This project studies a cipher, not a suspect.

Our objective is the cryptanalysis of Z13 as a text/cipher problem — never the identification of a suspect. We document publicly established case history and previously published claims, but we do not name new suspects, endorse an identification, or publish new accusatory claims about any living or identifiable private individual. A successful decryption, if one is ever achieved, is judged purely on cryptologic merit and does not by itself identify anyone. Read the full boundary ↗

Why Z13 is a different problem than Z408 and Z340

The two other Zodiac ciphers were solved. This one is 13 symbols long.

Z408 (408 symbols) was solved in about a week in 1969. Z340 (340 symbols) took 51 years but was solved in 2020 and confirmed by the FBI. Z13 is far shorter than either — short enough that this project's first real task is formally establishing whether a unique solution is even possible without an outside clue, not assuming it is because the other two were solved.
