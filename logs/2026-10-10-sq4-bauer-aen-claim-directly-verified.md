# SQ-4 — Bauer's "AEN" structural claim directly verified against the primary Z13 image

**Trigger:** actively searching for an unclaimed thread. Claim 1 in `data/sq4-prior-solution-claims-catalog.md`
(Bauer's "ALFRED E. NEUMAN" proposal) was recorded at search-synthesis tier only, with its central
structural claim — "Z13's first three cipher symbols decode to AEN" — never checked against this project's
own primary-source material. This project already has a directly-fetched, checksummed primary image of
the actual Z13 glyphs on file (`data/external-sources/wikimedia-z13-name-cipher-2026-09-27/`), acquired two
cycles before Claim 1 was even catalogued, for an unrelated purpose (confirming the repeat-position
pattern). The two were never cross-checked against each other until this cycle.

## What's already known / not done yet

Already known: the 2026-09-27 direct-view log recorded the 13-position sequence as "A, E, N, [crosshair],
[pinwheel], K, [pinwheel], M, [pinwheel], [hook], N, A, M" — using Latin-letter labels for several
positions, evidently because those specific glyphs visually resemble those letters, though the log itself
never explained why those particular labels were chosen or connected them to any outside claim (Bauer's
claim wasn't on file yet). Not done: re-opening the primary image with the specific question "does
Bauer's own stated evidentiary basis (shape resemblance) hold up under independent re-inspection."

## Design and why it's non-circular

The two pieces of evidence here were produced independently, two weeks apart, by different research
threads with no cross-reference: the primary-image labeling (2026-09-27, SQ-3, testing the repeat-pattern
claim) and Bauer's claim (2026-10-10, SQ-4, from external search). Neither informed the other at the time.
This cycle re-opens the already-checksummed primary PNG directly (not re-reading anyone's transcription)
and asks, independently of either prior record, whether the first three glyphs actually resemble Latin
"A," "E," "N" shapes — the specific, checkable claim both Bauer and this project's own 2026-09-27 labeling
implicitly make.

## Honesty precommitment

Report exactly what the glyphs look like, and do not extend this into a claim about what Z13 "means" or
who sent it — this is a structural, shape-level observation only, explicitly scoped to corroborating or
not corroborating Bauer's own stated evidence.

## Result

Directly re-opened `data/external-sources/wikimedia-z13-name-cipher-2026-09-27/z13-cipher-original.png`
(SHA256 `caf00fe7c7cb9643bf602b808bde6f2b0a4147433ecebe8829933ad63388008f`, already on file) and viewed it at
6x local upscaling. **Confirmed directly, independently of both the 2026-09-27 log's own labels and
Bauer's claim**: the first three glyphs are unambiguously shaped like the Latin letters **A**, **E**, and
**N** — not stylized, not ambiguous, straightforward upper-case letterforms drawn among otherwise-invented
symbols (a circled crosshair, a circled pinwheel, a hook shape). A fresh search confirms this is in fact
the entirety of Bauer's own stated evidentiary basis: a secondary source directly states "the A-E-N
letters are a reading of those shapes" — i.e. Bauer's claim is not a cryptanalytic derivation, it is this
exact shape-reading, now independently corroborated against the primary 1970 scan rather than taken on
Bauer's or any secondary source's word.

**Two further, non-repeat-structure positions also resemble Latin letters**, not previously connected to
Bauer's claim (which only addresses the first three): position 6 resembles "K" and position 8 resembles
"M." These are **reported descriptively only** — this project does not extend Bauer's reading into a new
proposed decipherment of its own; doing so would exceed SQ-4's own cataloging scope and risks exactly the
kind of speculative extension this project's standing discipline warns against.

**Positions 1=12, 3=11, 8=13 (the already-confirmed repeat pattern) are trivially self-consistent here**:
since these pairs are the same repeated glyph by construction, their matching letter-shapes (A=A, N=N,
M=M) confirm nothing new beyond what the repeat-pattern finding already established — flagged so this
isn't mistaken for four independent letter-shape confirmations when it's really one (positions 1-3,6,8)
plus their required repeats.

## Decision

Updating Claim 1 in `data/sq4-prior-solution-claims-catalog.md`: Bauer's stated evidentiary basis is now
**directly verified against this project's own primary-source image**, not merely reported from secondary
sources. This does not make the claim *correct* — Z13's own extreme shortness still makes any reading
statistically unfalsifiable by this project's own repeatedly-stated standard — but it does mean the claim's
specific, checkable premise (that three symbols look like A, E, N) survives direct primary-source scrutiny
rather than resting on an external cryptographer's unverified say-so. This is the project's first case of
cross-referencing its own two independently-produced research threads (SQ-3's primary-image work and
SQ-4's claims catalog) and finding they corroborate each other.
